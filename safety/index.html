Trust & safety

Practical steps for a safer campus exchange.

Arrange a public meetup

Pick a well-lit campus location during opening hours. Inspect the item and agree on its price before paying.

Keep personal details private

Use marketplace messages to coordinate. Keep your home address, passwords, payment codes and identity documents private.

Check what email confirmation means

Email confirmation shows that a member could access their inbox. College names are selected by members. Official enrollment is not verified.

Report suspicious listings

Use Report listing on an item’s page to send the moderation team the details.
